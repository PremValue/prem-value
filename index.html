⚡ Player Value for Money

Position-aware role score divided by market value — who gives the most for their price tag?

Role score = Position-weighted percentile score using output, expected output, defence, clean sheets, and saves
VFM Score = Role score ÷ Market Value (€M)
Bargain = Fresh value · Minutes ≥ 900 · Role score ≥ position median · Value ≤ position median
Stale value = Older than 180 days at the 2025-05-31 cutoff · visible but not featured
